Privacy Policy & Legal Terms

1. Anonymity & Data Privacy

Clean Streets is committed to protecting your identity. All reports are submitted anonymously:

  • EXIF data (camera, GPS, metadata) is automatically stripped before submission
  • Reports are filed under Clean Streets Inc., a registered legal entity
  • No personal identifying information is transmitted or stored
  • Your IP address and device information are not logged

2. Report Authenticity

False, AI-generated, or manipulated reports may result in:

  • Permanent ban from the platform
  • Legal action for filing false reports with authorities
  • Loss of community service hours and badges

3. Volunteer Liability

By participating in volunteer activities:

  • You assume all risks related to the cleanup activity
  • You release Clean Streets and municipal authorities from liability
  • You must follow all safety guidelines and use provided equipment

4. Image Rights

Photos you submit may be:

  • Used for verification and progress tracking
  • Displayed anonymously in public feeds
  • Used for AI training (with opt-out option)

5. AI Photo Verification

Clean Streets uses AI to verify photo authenticity for "before and after" cleanup logs. This includes detection of:

  • AI-generated images
  • Manipulated or doctored photos
  • Severely filtered or altered content

6. Community Service Hours

Community service hours are:

  • 1 hour per cleanup participation
  • Verified through photo documentation
  • Can be exported as official certificates

7. Platform Misuse

The platform reserves the right to:

  • Remove false or defamatory reports
  • Ban users who abuse the system
  • Report serious violations to authorities

Safe Cleanup Techniques

Personal Safety

  1. Always wear gloves
  2. Use safety vest for visibility
  3. Avoid sharp objects
  4. Wash hands after cleanup

Efficient Collection

  1. Use grabber tools for reaching
  2. Sort waste on-site when possible
  3. Fill bags gradually
  4. Keep pathways clear

Proper Disposal

  1. Segregate as instructed
  2. Tie bags securely
  3. Use designated bins
  4. Dispose safely

Area Restoration

  1. Sweep thoroughly
  2. Check for hidden litter
  3. Document before/after
  4. Leave area cleaner

What is a Pothole?

Definition: A hole or depression in the road surface caused by water infiltration and traffic damage.

Why It Matters: Potholes cause accidents, vehicle damage, and water pooling.

How to Fix: Professional road patching with asphalt. Requires municipal action.

Action: Report to Government

Dark/Unlit Streets

Definition: Areas lacking adequate street lighting, affecting safety.

Why It Matters: Increases crime, accidents, and reduces foot traffic.

How to Fix: Installation of street lights and electrical work. Requires municipal action.

Action: Report to Government

Drainage Issues

Definition: Blocked or faulty drainage causing water pooling and overflow.

Why It Matters: Leads to flooding, disease spread, and property damage.

How to Fix: Professional drain cleaning and repair. Requires municipal action.

Action: Report to Government

Plastic Waste Accumulation

Definition: Build-up of plastic materials in public spaces.

Why It Matters: Environmental hazard, impacts wildlife, pollutes soil.

How to Fix: Community cleanup and proper segregation. Volunteers can help!

Action: Ask for Volunteers

Roadside Dirt & Debris

Definition: Accumulated dirt, leaves, and debris on roadsides.

Why It Matters: Affects aesthetics and can harbor disease vectors.

How to Fix: Regular sweeping and debris collection. Community can help!

Action: Ask for Volunteers

Park Maintenance Issues

Definition: Parks lacking proper upkeep, cleanliness, or maintenance.

Why It Matters: Reduces community usage and quality of life.

How to Fix: Community-led cleanup initiatives. Get volunteers involved!

Action: Ask for Volunteers
